The mark
160 · 64 · 32 · 16

Dark frame, paper inset, one orange light. It is the shell from every page. At 16 px the "s" goes and the light stays; three shapes still read.

Construction · 180 grid

Frame radius is 12% of the side. Paper inset is 8%. Header is 12%. LED is centred in the header band, 17% in from the right, 7% wide. Scale the grid, never redraw.

Rules
Always three parts: frame, paper, light. Nothing else goes inside. Light is orange, top right, and never turns green or red in the logo. State lives in the app, not the mark. Wordmark is Caveat 600 in paper ink on hardware. Never on paper, never in another face. Clear space around the mark equals the paper inset. Never crop the frame. On light backgrounds the frame is the edge. On dark ones add a 1 px paper ring so the frame does not sink.
