# Design decisions

A ledger so settled choices are not re-argued. Newest first. Each entry says what was decided, why, and what it replaced. The mockups live on the [design canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/E37216htrsgRJFEVwhshDc) and as source in [`canvas/`](canvas/).

| Date | Decision | Why | Replaces |
|---|---|---|---|
| 22 Sep 2026 | An entry only offers to open when there is something behind it, and a captured one offers "Yes, that's right" and "See what you said". | `has_more_info` exists so opening is not a round trip to find out there is nothing, and it was read by nobody: nine rows in eleven offered more info and opened to "No more info on this one". Agreeing is the only thing that promotes a captured memory, and since unconfirmed ones stopped loading at session start there was no way to promote one from the app at all. | "Read more info" on every row; no confirm anywhere in the web app; the source never shown. |
| 22 Sep 2026 | Forget stops deleting. It ends a memory, which stops it loading and puts it in a new archive at `/book/archive` with Put it back. Deleting for good moves there and is the only destructive act in Satchel. | The button's own confirmation already promised "it leaves active retrieval right away" while the code ran a DELETE that also destroyed the row's history. Consolidation now applies itself unwatched on the argument that nothing it does is destructive, and a person whose own Forget was the one irreversible act had that guarantee backwards. | Forget as a hard delete, with no archive and no way back. |
| 22 Sep 2026 | A memory has a kind, chosen when it is written: how things are, how you like things, something you want. Shown on every entry, not editable on a correction. | The kind decides the lifetime and whether anything may ever retire the memory. Only something you wanted can be finished. Nothing but the person writing it knows which it is, and the database had the column with no way to set it. | Every memory saved from the web app being a `fact` by default. |
| 22 Sep 2026 | Developer mode: a switch in Settings that adds a seventh rail destination, Activity, showing every request, document and memory change in one stream. Per browser, read-only. | Six tables answer six questions and none answers "what happened". Telling a bad retrieval from a bad capture from a bad consolidation meant four queries and comparing timestamps by hand. The switch keeps the rail at six for everyone who does not ask for it. | The rail being six fixed destinations with no exception, and "correction history view" being v2 with nothing in its place. |
| 17 Sep 2026 | Moving a task to Blocked always records a progress update with status `blocked` and the reason as the body. One call, one transaction. | The timeline must always show why. A checkbox to opt in was one more thing to forget. | A separate transition call plus an optional progress note. |
| 17 Sep 2026 | No "Delete this Satchel" in v1 Settings. | Not needed yet. Export covers taking your data out. | A danger-zone row in the Settings mockup. |
| 17 Sep 2026 | Real URL routing for every destination and detail page. | Back, refresh and links must work. The rail, picker, task detail and search all need addresses. | View held in React state; reload lands on Memory. |
| 17 Sep 2026 | "Resume here" is removed. Buttons say "Open task". The strongest action on a task is "Copy handoff". | Satchel holds context. It does not act on it yet. A button that promises to launch work breaks that. | Resume buttons on Left off, task detail and the phone reference. |
| 17 Sep 2026 | Skills are descoped and removed from the project for now. | No skills feature exists in the app. | A Skills destination in the screen map. |
| 17 Sep 2026 | Dark theme is v2. v1 ships light only. Settings has no Appearance row. | Ship one theme well first. | Light/Dark/Match device segment in Settings. |
| 17 Sep 2026 | Phone is v2 and will be a native Android app with app intents, so the Gemini assistant can create tasks and add memories. The web app is desktop-first. | A phone web layout would be thrown away. The intents make name, description and next action the whole contract. | Responsive phone breakpoints in the web app. |
| 18 Sep 2026 | The mark is the shell itself: dark frame, paper inset, one orange light, a Caveat "s" on the paper. The wordmark stays Caveat 600 on hardware. Both share the same three parts. Files in [mark/](mark/). | Logo and product look the same because they are the same. Reads at 16 px with the glyph dropped. Sits next to the wordmark without a second voice. | A plain bag, a strap-shaped S, a notebook with a band. See the Mark board. |
| 17 Sep 2026 | Handwriting lives in one place: the wordmark, set in Caveat 600 on the hardware. All headings stay Newsreader. | A script H1 read fine on one-word pages and wrong on the consent page. Two written voices fought on one page. Thin scripts faded on dark paper. | Options A (script H1 everywhere) and B (script for date and provenance lines). See the Headings board. |
| 18 Sep 2026 | On the connected page each half says one thing. Left: “Codex can now read your Satchel”, only what you allowed, revoke in Apps. Right: “Go back to Codex”, memory loads in the next chat. No scope list, it is an at-a-glance page. | Both halves said “connected” with a headline and a lede each, so the page read twice. | Both halves restating the connection. |
| 18 Sep 2026 | The connected page right half now uses the partner’s colour, not white: Claude clay `#D97757` with a white Claude-style mark behind the text, Codex black with a faint grey OpenAI-style mark. The marks are the real vector paths (Claude from Simple Icons, Codex from its app icon). Check the partners’ brand terms before a public launch. | The white right half read as unfinished and did not say which app you came from. | The plain ivory and black halves with no marks (17 Sep). |
| 17 Sep 2026 | The connected page after consent is a plain acknowledgement split down the middle: Satchel's look on the left, the partner's on the right. Real logos are not used. | Simple ack. Copying another company's marks is not ours to do. | A busier version with terminal cards and scope grids. |
| 17 Sep 2026 | Consent page shows Memory and Tasks side by side with Select all / None per group and a quick-start row. | Ticking projects one by one does not scale. | One checkbox per project, stacked. |
| 17 Sep 2026 | Hardware is the outer box and paints everything. Paper is an inset panel with its own smaller radius. | Fixes the light corners on the live site, where paper painted under the shell's dark border. Interim one-line fix: `background-clip: padding-box` on `.shell`. | One box with a thick dark border and paper background. |
| 17 Sep 2026 | Scope is a picker, not chips or a sidebar list. One control on Book and Tasks: search, For me pinned, projects with counts, New project at the bottom. Locks while a draft is open and says why. | Works for 3 projects and for 40. | Project buttons in a left sidebar; chips in the older prototype. |
| 17 Sep 2026 | Six destinations in a left rail with horizontal labels: Left off, Book, Tasks, Projects, Apps, Settings. | The review flagged rotated labels. Three header buttons did not scale. | Header buttons for Memory, Tasks, Connections. |
| 17 Sep 2026 | Empty states follow one rule: say what the place is for, give the one action that fills it, offer a look at the full version. | The live app greets a new user with a form and a sentence. | Single-sentence empty states. |
| 17 Sep 2026 | Every record shows provenance: who saved it, from where, when, revision. History is a link, not struck-through text in the list. | The review found struck-through history crowds the list. | Struck-through corrections in the prototype. |
| 17 Sep 2026 | Errors appear in place with a red border and plain words. Success readouts go in the footer, not toasts. | Toasts vanish. In-place text says what failed, what is safe, and one thing to do. | Inline notice blocks with a left border. |
| 17 Sep 2026 | Motion: one curve, three durations (120, 180, 320 ms). Only the verify light animates on its own, once. Reduced motion turns everything into a swap. | Physical controls should feel pressed, not bounced. | No motion system. |
| 8 Sep 2026 | Direction is a personal notebook with physical controls. Paper for what you wrote, hardware for what apps may do. Serif book pairing: Newsreader, Instrument Sans, DM Mono. | See [visual-direction.md](visual-direction.md). | Forest and cobalt explorations in `archive/`. |
